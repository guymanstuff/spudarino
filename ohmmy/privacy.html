Privacy Policy

Ohm My! · Last updated November 23, 2026

Ohm My! is made by Spudarino ("we"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version: Ohm My! does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

Information the app uses

  • Battery and device status. The app reads your battery level, charging state, Low Power Mode status and device temperature state to display them. This information is shown on screen only and is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your preferences. Your theme, appearance, checklist progress and shortcut names are saved on your device so the app remembers them. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking
  • No network requests: the app does not connect to the internet
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone

Shortcuts

When you tap Run, Ohm My! asks Apple's Shortcuts app to run a shortcut you created. What that shortcut does is controlled by you and by Apple's Shortcuts app, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us or visit the Ohm My! support page.
