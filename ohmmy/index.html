Ohm My!

Make your battery last longer.

Ohm My! shows your battery status at a glance, switches power profiles in one tap using Apple's Shortcuts app, and walks you through the settings that make the biggest difference to battery life.

Frequently asked questions

Tapping Run says the shortcut can't be found.

Each profile runs a shortcut you create once in the Shortcuts app, and the name has to match exactly, including capitals and spaces. Tap the ⓘ button next to the profile to see the expected name, follow the setup steps, or change the name to match a shortcut you already have.

Why doesn't Ohm My! change my settings directly?

iOS doesn't allow App Store apps to change system settings such as Low Power Mode, brightness or Bluetooth. Apple's Shortcuts app can, so Ohm My! runs your shortcuts for one-tap changes and shows you exactly where to find everything else in Settings.

Why does tapping Run open the Shortcuts app?

That's how iOS runs shortcuts from other apps. The shortcut runs, then you can switch back. You can also add your profile shortcuts to your Home Screen or Control Center from the Shortcuts app.

Can profiles switch on automatically?

Yes. Open the Guide tab for step-by-step instructions on Shortcuts automations, such as switching to Max Saver when your battery drops below 30% or back to Normal when you plug in.

A Settings location in the checklist doesn't match my device.

Apple occasionally renames or moves settings between iOS versions. Let us know which one using the contact link above, and we'll update it.

Does Ohm My! collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing leaves your device. See the privacy policy.
